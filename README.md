# studious-parakeet
A documentation of my Cybersecurity Industrial Training (IT) experience, including weekly learning activities, practical exercises, assignments, security tools, and projects completed throughout 2026.
1. CIA Triad

The CIA Triad is a cybersecurity model consisting of three principles: Confidentiality: protecting information from unauthorized access, Integrity: ensuring information remains accurate and unaltered, and Availability: ensuring information is accessible when needed.

2. Cybersecurity Risk

Cybersecurity risk is the possibility of loss, damage, or disruption to computer systems, networks, or data due to cyber threats exploiting vulnerabilities. I learned about threats, vulnerabilities, likelihood, impact, risk management, and risk treatment methods.

3. OSINT (Open-Source Intelligence)

OSINT is the process of collecting, analyzing, and interpreting information from publicly available sources such as search engines, social media, websites, and public records. I also learned about OSINT tools such as WHOIS, Wayback Machine, crt.sh, and WhatsMyName.
